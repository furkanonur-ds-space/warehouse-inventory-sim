# Run results, 2026-08-27

A complete copy of `out/` as this run left it, and the first flight with the
barcode reader running beside the scan. `out/` stays gitignored - every run
rewrites it - so this folder is the copy that survives: nothing here is
regenerated by the next flight.

Open the three HTML files directly in a browser. They are self-contained and
need no network.

## The barcode side

`perception/barcode_scanner.py` ran in its own process for the whole flight,
subscribed to the same camera topic and touching nothing in `scanner/`. It
processed 10787 frames and decoded 2616 CODE128 readings, of which 930 could be
tied to a QR visible in the same frame; those covered 266 of the 432 boxes.

Every one of those 266 placards named the same slot the box's own QR was filed
under, and none disagreed. That is what the placard is for: its payload names a
SLOT, not a box, so it cannot identify a product, but it is an independent
confirmation of the shelf, level and bay the scan chose.

The 142 payloads that could not be linked are readings where no QR decoded in
that frame. They are recorded rather than dropped, because a placard read with
nothing to tie it to is still evidence the vehicle was in front of that bay.

## The four boxes this run missed

`missed_boxes.jsonl` now covers four runs. One box, `F-02-L3`, has been missed
in all four. `D-06-L1` and `F-02-L1` have been missed in three. None of the
three is explained by geometry: all sit within the camera's 0.23 m vertical
half-frame, at 3.88 px per QR module, which decodes.

What did come out of this run is a lead. The scanning camera sits 10 cm forward
of `base_link` at body height, and the vehicle's own front propellers sit 6 cm
above it, inside the lens cone. Two frames grabbed 12 seconds apart show five
dark shapes at identical pixel positions while the whole scene behind them
changed: the propellers block about 34% of the frame width in a band on the
optical axis. The repeat offenders are exactly the boxes whose labels sit at
`+0.00 m` off that axis. Moving the camera forward or down would clear the
band, but that is a change to the vehicle model and has not been made.
